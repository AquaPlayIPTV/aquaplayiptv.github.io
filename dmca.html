AquaPlay is a neutral technology tool: a media player. We do not provide, host, sell, index, link to or distribute TV channels, movies, playlists or IPTV subscriptions, and we have no relationship with any IPTV provider.

Streams and playlists

Every stream a user plays comes from a playlist or account the user added, served by a third party. None of it passes through, or is stored on, any server of ours, so there is nothing for us to take down. A notice about a stream belongs with the provider that serves it and that provider's host.
