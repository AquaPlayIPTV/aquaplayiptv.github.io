Everything AquaPlay does

Live TV with the full guide, up to 14 days of catch-up, films and series, subtitles in your language, and your phone as the TV's keyboard. All of it with the remote's arrows, OK and Back.

A guide that fills the screen

  • A full TV guide as a grid, beside the picture, or as a TV Catalogue over several days, as far ahead as your provider's guide goes.
  • Three layouts for the channel list: classic, grid, or beside the picture.
  • Programme info with its description, which scrolls by itself at the speed you choose.
  • Search across channels and programmes, and reminders before a show starts.
  • Groups and favourites: favourite channels and favourite groups, recently watched, show, hide, reorder and pin groups.
  • Your channel order: sort by number or A to Z, move and hide channels, and give them your own numbers.
  • Channel versions (FHD, HD, backup) kept together, and the stream quality you pick for each channel, with a fallback if it will not play.
  • Hold OK on a channel for its menu; hold Left or Right to change group; CH+ and CH− can turn a page of the list.
  • Your remote, your way: choose what the arrow keys do and set your own buttons.
  • A clock on full screen, in the corner you choose; full screen picks up where you left it.
  • Your guide, matched: use your provider's guide or your own guide address, match a channel to its guide by hand, and take channel logos from the playlist or the guide.
  • Guide memory sizes (Normal, Large, Largest, Custom) for very big guides, and a choice of how many programme descriptions are kept.

Missed it? Play it from the start

  • Replay past shows from up to 14 days back, where your provider keeps them.
  • Start over a show that is on now.
  • Rewind live TV and jump back to live.
  • A catch-up list for each channel, with what you picked from it kept in view.

A cinema shelf from your own account

  • Movies and Series on screens of their own, from your Xtream Codes account, with categories you can show, hide and pin, and Recently added.
  • A details page for every film: backdrop, story, cast, director and Play.
  • Series pages with seasons and a row of episode cards; Continue and Up next take you to the right episode.
  • Watched marks on films and episodes, set by hand or as you finish them.
  • Simkl marks what you finish on your Simkl list.
  • Skip intro with times from TheIntroDB, or an intro you mark yourself.
  • Playback speed and scrubbing through a film.

Subtitles in your language, on by themselves

  • Your language, on by itself: live channels and films switch subtitles on in the language you pick, including DVB subtitles and the TV's own captions on Samsung.
  • Subtitles found online when a film has none in your language.
  • Subtitle size and look, and a delay to move them earlier or later.
  • Audio and subtitle tracks named by their language, even when the stream names them oddly.

Your look, your profiles, your locks

  • Aqua, OLED and Custom themes, with accent colours.
  • Three text sizes and 17 languages, Hebrew and Arabic right to left.
  • Profiles ("Who's watching"), each with its own favourites and history.
  • A parental PIN and channel locks.
  • Picture size and a sleep timer.

The TV's keyboard is your phone

  • Add a playlist (M3U link or file, Xtream Codes login, Stalker/MAG portal) by typing the TV's code on your phone; for Stalker/MAG, AquaPlay can make the MAC address for you.
  • Several playlists at once, each one editable.
  • Send your playlists and settings to another TV, and back up to your phone and restore from it.
  • Encrypted on the phone before anything leaves it; only your TV can read it.

Steady playback on your connection

  • Stream format Auto, MPEG-TS or HLS; if HLS keeps stalling, AquaPlay offers to switch.
  • Buffer size to suit your line, and a warning when the stream keeps running dry.
  • Reconnects by itself after a drop, and opens a channel again if it starts without picture.
  • A connection speed check, and a data saver that caps the quality, by hand or automatically.

Kept up to date, and easy to get help

  • What's new after every update.
  • On Samsung, AquaPlay tells you when a new version is out.
  • A crash report and Share my settings, each opened on your phone with a code, sent only if you send it.
  • Support AquaPlay on Ko-fi, and an About page.
