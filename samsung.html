AquaPlay for Samsung TVs

Free, with every feature, on Samsung Smart TVs from 2017 on (Tizen 3.0 or later). You install it once from your computer with Apps2Samsung.

See the five steps Download AquaPlay for free (.wgt)

Install in five steps.

You need the TV and a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux) on the same network.

  1. 01

    Turn on Developer mode on the TV

    Open Apps, then press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote (or the on-screen number pad). Switch Developer mode on, type your computer's IP address, choose OK, and restart the TV: hold the power button until it switches off and on again.

  2. 02

    Get Apps2Samsung on your computer

    Download it from github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung and open it. It finds your TV.

  3. 03

    Download AquaPlay for free

    The .wgt file from the latest release, downloaded at once.

  4. 04

    Install it with Apps2Samsung

    Choose your TV, choose to install your own package, and pick the AquaPlay file. The first time, Apps2Samsung may ask you to sign in to a Samsung account, to make the certificate your TV needs.

  5. 05

    Open AquaPlay and add your playlist

    Find it in the TV's Apps, and add your playlist from the remote or from your phone.

New versions.

AquaPlay tells you when a new version is out, with a code that opens this page. From version 1.0.54 it downloads the new version itself, checks it, and asks you to restart.

To update by hand, download the new .wgt and install it the same way, from the same computer. It replaces the old one and keeps your playlists and settings.

A copy installed from a different computer was signed with a different certificate, and the TV will not install over it. Back up to your phone first (AquaPlay's Settings), then delete AquaPlay on the TV (Apps, then the settings gear) and install again.

What you get.

The guide, catch-up, films and series, subtitles in your language, profiles, and your phone as the keyboard. The same app as on Android TV, which adds multi-view and recording.
